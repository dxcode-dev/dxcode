You can now clean up projects and workspaces you no longer need.

## Delete a project

Open the project's settings and, on **General**, choose **Delete Project**. Pick what happens to its Threads:

- **Keep them archived.** Each person keeps the Threads they started, read-only. They can never be unarchived.
- **Delete them**, with their conversations and sandboxes.

Type the project's name to confirm. The project leaves every list, and its name is free to use again. You can delete a private project you own. In a workspace, the project's creator or a workspace owner or admin can delete it.

## Delete or leave a workspace

As a workspace's owner, you see **Delete Workspace** in **Workspace Settings → Workspace**, with the same choice for the Threads in its projects. Type the workspace's short name to confirm. Deleting a workspace removes its projects, members, and invites. dx also asks each provider to revoke the workspace's source connections and every sign-in to the workspace's MCP servers.

To leave a workspace you own without deleting it, hand it on first: on **Members**, choose **Transfer Ownership** for another member. They become the owner, and you become an admin who can leave.
