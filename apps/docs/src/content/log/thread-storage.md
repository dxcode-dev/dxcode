---
title: Thread storage keeps the agent's screenshots and videos
date: 2026-10-27
summary: Agents store files with their Thread, and replies show stored images and videos inline, in a gallery.
kind: news
---

The images and videos an agent shows you no longer vanish when its Orb goes away.

An agent stores files with its Thread using `dxd files put`, and links them in its reply. Stored images and videos show inline, and several in a row become a gallery you can page through or open full screen.

Stored files stay readable after the Orb sleeps, stops, or is destroyed, and while the Thread is archived. Anyone who can read the Thread can open them. They are deleted with the Thread.
